# Our logo

Domolux is the next-generation home automation interface, offering a refined and powerful experience.

![Logo](/images/brand/logo.png)

# Design

The Domolux logo features a distinctive circular emblem that represents the connected home ecosystem — modern, elegant, and intelligent.

### Dark Elegance

The deep black foundation conveys sophistication, stability, and premium quality. It anchors the brand with a sense of reliability and timelessness.

### Gold Accents

Subtle gold and warm beige tones add a touch of refinement, suggesting value, warmth, and a premium user experience. These accents elevate the brand without overpowering its minimalist character.

### Circular Form

The circular shape symbolizes unity, completeness, and the all-encompassing nature of a connected home. It represents the seamless integration of technology into everyday life.

# Usage

The default variation is the colored wordmark on a light background.

## Color variations, backgrounds, and placement

Use the logo on light backgrounds by default. On dark backgrounds, ensure sufficient contrast.

Do not enclose the logo in a square or any confined backgrounds, except in specific situations enforced by another company's marketplace guidelines, for example, an iOS app icon.

Do not add drop shadow to the logo. If legibility is compromised due to the background, change the background to provide more contrast.

## Exclusion zone

The logo needs some personal space. Its exclusion zone is equal to a quarter the height of the icon.

## Animation

The default is the static variant.

Use the animated variant only for introductory purposes, for example, in the beginning of a video or on a loading screen.

Do not repeat the logo animation.
